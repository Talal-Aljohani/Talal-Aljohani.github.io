Gravity tracking replaces both filters and is the better answer: it follows where “down” points using the gyro, so a chassis that rolls mid-run is told apart from a real sideways force. A high-pass cannot make that distinction — it removes every sustained term, including the car leaning on a rail. Untick tracking to fall back to the old offset-and-filter chain.

A viewing flip, applied on top of the automatic axis correction above: the car drifts the other way and the nose turns with it. Use it only to match the side you filmed from — a camera at the finish line sees left and right the other way round. Speed and distance are unaffected.
